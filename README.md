SOIL DRILLING VEHICLE

Project Description

In earlier days, soil digging for plantation was carried out manually using traditional tools such as hand augers. This process required considerable time, physical effort, and human labour, especially for large-scale plantation activities.

To overcome these difficulties, this project presents a Soil Drilling Vehicle designed to reduce manual labour and improve the efficiency of agricultural plantation.

The vehicle consists of a petrol engine-powered drilling mechanism and a battery-operated electric drive system. The engine is used to rotate the auger drill bit and operate its vertical movement mechanism. The vehicle movement is controlled by battery-powered DC motors, allowing the machine to move from one location to another.

This combination of mechanical and electrical systems provides an efficient solution for soil drilling and plantation activities.

Objectives

- To reduce manual soil digging work.
- To save time during plantation.
- To reduce human effort.
- To develop a mobile soil drilling vehicle.
- To provide an efficient drilling and vertical movement mechanism.
- To use battery-powered DC motors for vehicle movement.

Working Principle

The vehicle operates using two separate power systems.

1. Drilling and Vertical Movement System

A petrol engine provides power to the drilling mechanism. It rotates the auger drill bit to penetrate the soil. The engine-powered mechanism also provides the vertical movement required to lower and raise the drilling assembly.

2. Vehicle Drive System

A rechargeable battery supplies electrical energy to DC motors. These motors drive the vehicle wheels, allowing the vehicle to move forward and backward.

Main Components

- Petrol Engine
- Auger Drill Bit
- Vertical Movement Mechanism
- Rechargeable Battery
- DC Motors
- Wheels
- Motor Controller
- Vehicle Frame and Supporting Structure
- Connecting Wires

Applications

- Agricultural plantation
- Sapling plantation
- Gardening
- Small-scale farming
- Soil drilling for planting operations
